# Sales Analysis – Business Overview

## Business Questions

1. Who are our top 10 customers by sales?

2. Which products generate the highest revenue?

3. Which product categories are growing the fastest?

4. Which customer segment buys the most?

5. Which regions have declining sales?

6. What is the average order value?

# 2. Which products generate the highest revenue?

## Key Findings

The highest revenue-generating products are:

- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier

- Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch Plastic Comb Binding Machine with Manual Bind

The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier contributes approximately **38%** of the revenue among the top-performing products, while the Fellowes PB500 contributes around **11%**.

## Insights

Revenue is heavily concentrated in a few products, particularly the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier. Maintaining inventory availability and promoting complementary products can help maximize sales while reducing dependency on a single product.


# 3. Which product categories are growing the fastest?

## Key Findings

Several product categories showed positive month-over-month (MoM) sales growth during the analysis period, indicating increasing customer demand.

## Insights

Categories with consistent MoM growth should be prioritized for inventory planning, marketing campaigns, and promotional activities. Monitoring declining categories can also help identify products that require pricing adjustments or targeted promotions.


# 4. Which customer segment buys the most?

## Key Findings

The **Consumer** segment generated the highest total sales among all customer segments.

## Insights

The Consumer segment is the primary revenue driver for the business. Focusing marketing efforts, customer engagement, and promotional campaigns on this segment can further increase sales and customer retention.


# 5. Which regions have declining sales?

## Key Findings

The **South** and **East** regions experienced declining sales during the most recent month.

## Insights

The declining sales trend in these regions may indicate changing customer demand, increased competition, or operational challenges. Further investigation is recommended to identify the root causes and implement corrective actions.


# 6. What is the average order value?

## Key Findings

The average order value (AOV) is **$229**.

## Insights

An average order value of $229 indicates the typical amount customers spend per transaction. Increasing this metric through cross-selling, upselling, and bundled product offers can improve overall revenue without acquiring additional customers.
